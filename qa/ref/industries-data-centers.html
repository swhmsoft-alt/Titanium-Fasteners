Data centers are the nerve centers of today’s digital economy. They power cloud services, connect global enterprises, and enable the rapid adoption of AI, 5G, and edge computing. 

With this transformation come new engineering challenges: rising rack densities, continuous uptime requirements, and increasing pressure to improve energy efficiency.

At Bossard, we deliver fastening, cooling, and cable management solutions engineered for the unique demands of modern data centers, helping operators and OEMs achieve higher performance, lower downtime, and scalable growth.

Engineering Pain Points in the Data Center Industry

The rapid growth of data centers brings immense challenges in efficiency, reliability, and scalability. Bossard helps solve five critical issues faced by operators and OEMs:

Thermal Management

Rising compute density and power consumption make heat one of the biggest threats to data center performance. Inefficient thermal management can cause hotspots, reduce component lifespan, and drive up energy costs. We support both air and liquid cooling systems with fastening and sealing solutions designed to maintain even pressure during thermal expansion and contraction, improving heat transfer and ensuring stable operation across servers, racks, and cooling assemblies.

Overlay

Mechanical Stress

Continuous vibration from fans, pumps, and power equipment can loosen joints and compromise structural integrity. Over time, this leads to premature wear or system failure. Our vibration-resistant and fatigue-proof fastening technologies, such as self-locking screws, washers, and engineered inserts are designed to withstand dynamic loads and maintain joint stability in high-vibration environments, helping data centers run longer with fewer maintenance interventions.

Overlay

Maintenance and Downtime

Every minute of downtime impacts productivity and operational cost. Complex assemblies or hard to reach components make maintenance time-consuming and risky. Bossard provides quick-release, captive, and tool-less fastening systems that simplify access, speed up component replacement, and reduce human error. Standardized parts across global sites further streamline inventory and servicing, maximizing uptime and operational efficiency.

Overlay

Signal and Power Reliability

In high-density data centers, stable signal transmission and power continuity are essential. Poor grounding, EMI interference, or micro-loosening can lead to voltage drops and data errors. We deliver conductive fasteners, grounding screws, and EMI-shielded components that ensure electrical integrity in PDUs, racks, and cable systems, protecting sensitive electronics from performance loss or failure.

Overlay

Scalability and Supply Chain

As operators expand globally, maintaining consistency in quality and part availability becomes a major challenge. Fragmented supply chains can slow deployment and increase costs. Our global presence, combined with our Smart Factory Logistics solutions ensure standardized fasteners and components are available worldwide, supporting modular construction, rapid scaling, and predictable supply performance across all data center locations.

Server Chassis

Server chassis are the foundation of every data center, housing processor, GPU, and memory module that run 24/7. As rack densities increase, they must stay structurally stable, easy to service, and optimized for airflow. Bossard ensures your chassis assemblies deliver strength, vibration resistance, and serviceability without compromising uptime.   • Self-Clinching Standoffs and Pins
  • Captive Panel Screws
  • Riveting Solutions
  • Right-Angle Fasteners
  • Adhesive-Backed Cable Mounts
  • Handles and Latches

Motherboard and PCB Solutions

Motherboards and PCBs form the nervous system of every server, carrying CPU, GPU, and high-speed memory. Even small fastening failures can lead to flex, signal degradation, or thermal issues. Therefore we offer precision fasteners that protect sensitive boards, enable automated assembly, and ensure long-term stability under load. Typical fastening solutions include:

  • Threaded Standoffs and Spacers
  • Surface-Mount Fasteners
  • Precision Screws
  • High-Current Power Fasteners
  • Specialty Board-Level Fasteners

Power Distributions and Backup Power

Uninterrupted power is mission-critical for every data center. Failures in PDUs or backup systems can cascade across hundreds of servers, risking downtime and safety hazards. Our fastening and grounding solutions secure high-current connections and ensure compliance, reliability, and safe servicing of electrical infrastructure. Reliable fastening and grounding elements for safe, uninterrupted power distribution include:

  • High-Current Power Fasteners
  • Locking Power Cords and Straps
  • Anti-Loosening Fastening Solutions
  • Grounding and Bonding Hardware
  • Cable Clamps and Strain Relief Components
