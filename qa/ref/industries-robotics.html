Fasteners for Industrial Robots

Driving the Future of Manufacturing through Automation

Industrial robots are used for heavy duty tasks like assembly, handling, palletizing, spraying, and welding. The fasteners required for these robots need to be tough, vibration-resistant, highly durable, and able to offer consistent quality with strong corrosion resistance.

Fasteners for Collaborative Robots

Human-Machine Interaction

Collaborative robots are equipped with special safety features since they operate alongside humans. The fasteners used for these robots need to be easy to assemble and disassemble, meet strict safety standards, and offer both vibration resistance and consistent quality.
