Reliable Fastening Solutions for Railway Technology

The railway sector is characterized by a high demand for reliability, speed, punctuality, and safety. To enhance train efficiency and reduce costs, there is an increasing use of lightweight materials.

Additionally, quick production times and lean maintenance are essential, creating a unique demand for fasteners. At Bossard, we understand these trends and the challenges they bring, which is why we offer railway-specific fastening solutions that adhere to the highest safety and quality standards. Rail-Specific Fasteners for Different Applications

Supply Units in Rail Vehicles

For the supply units in railcars, the comfort of the passengers as well as a fast accessibility for maintenance and inspection of the equipment are paramount. The connecting elements must, above all, be quickly replaceable, captive, reusable for assembly and disassembly, and suitable for easy and safe mounting.

  • Cable glands 
  • Compression latches 
  • Hose clamps 
  • High-strength threaded inserts 
  • Blind rivet nuts 
  • Self-Clinching Fasteners

Railcar Body

In this key application, structural stability and reliability are vital for ensuring passenger safety. That's why we recommend fasteners that are easy to assemble and disassemble, made from high-strength materials with excellent corrosion resistance, all while meeting stringent safety standards.

  • Slotted self locking nuts 
  • Lockbolts 
  • Blind rivet nuts 
  • Grounding studs 
  • Tensioner nuts

Bogie in Rail Vehicles

The bogie plays a vital role in the train. Its main requirements are operating reliability, maintainability and traveling comfort through sufficient suspension and damping. Fasteners must, above all, be vibration-proof, corrosion resistant, resistant to extreme temperature changes, and able to maintain clamp force of assembly.

  • Lock washers 
  • Wedge locking washers 
  • Hex head locking screws 
  • Tension control bolts

Interior of Railcar Vehicles

Luggage racks, grab poles, seats, and folding tables need to endure daily wear and tear. To meet these demands, we provide fasteners suitable for various materials, available in different colors, and designed to fulfill high standards of design, functionality, and lightweight construction.

  • Hinges 
  • Fasteners for composite materials 
  • Threaded inserts 
  • Fasteners for C-rails 
  • High strength blind rivets 
  • Cable tie plates 
  • Pan head screws with flange 
  • Notched screws 
  • Blind rivet nuts 
  • Self-Clinching fasteners

Energy Supply of Railcar Vehicles

Fasteners in this application must meet the highest safety standards. Along with ensuring a secure connection, fire protection is a critical factor. Therefore, the key requirements for fastening solutions include corrosion resistance, vibration resistance, and effective fire protection.

  • Barb cable ties 
  • Wrap sleevings 
  • Resilient conical washers 
  • Coatings 
  • Blind rivet nuts
