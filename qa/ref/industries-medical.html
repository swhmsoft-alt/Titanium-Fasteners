Fasteners for Electromedical Devices in the Operating Room

Fastening Solutions for MRI, Ultrasound, X-Ray, and Surgical Lights

Operating room equipment must perform flawlessly with zero tolerance for failure. Fasteners need to be safe yet innovative, with high strength, vibration resistance, and corrosion protection.

  • Securing and anti-loosening screws 
  • Securing and anti-loosening washers 
  • Direct assembly screws for plastics 
  • Direct assembly screws for metal 
  • Retaining rings 
  • Lockbolts 
  • Embedding and surface bonding fasteners 
  • Levelling elements 
  • Cable glands 
  • Sealing and protection plugs 
  • Machining (turned and milled parts) 
  • Gas springs and damper solutions

Fasteners for Surgical and Therapy Equipment in Intensive Care Units

Ideal for Hospital Beds, Infusion Pumps, Wheelchairs, Dialysis Machines, and Ventilators

In intensive care units, where critical care equipment must meet stringent standards, fasteners play a key role. They need to be vibration- and corrosion-resistant, comply with safety regulations, and be made from high-strength materials.

  • Self-clinching nuts 
  • Self-clinching threaded studs 
  • Self-clinching threaded standoffs 
  • Blind rivet nuts 
  • Blind rivets 
  • Structural blind rivets 
  • Speed rivets 
  • Threaded inserts 
  • Sealing plugs 
  • Mobility and castor wheels 
  • Positioning elements 
  • Latching 
  • Indexing elements
