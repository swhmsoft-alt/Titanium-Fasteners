Fastening Composites

Innovative Fastening Solutions for Composite Materials

Fastening composites is challenging because unlike traditional materials, there are no standard guidelines. We offer expert guidance on selecting the right fastening technology, from initial design, through manufacturing to final assembly.

Connecting the Dots: Our Approach in 2 Minutes

Fastening technologies, products, latest engineering know-how, and proven productivity for fastening composites

Our Valued Customers

We Solve Your Fastening Challenge

Bossard has the most effective composite fastening technologies available. Beyond that, our expertise lies in precisely specifying when and how to use them. We eliminate guesswork and mitigate risks at every stage of your design, manufacturing, and assembly processes.

We Guide – Eliminating Trial and Error

We ensure seamless collaboration between Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM) and producers to achieve optimal results. In doing so, we pay attention to the correct functionality of composite materials and the integration of appropriate technologies into the manufacturing process. Our approach begins with the creation of a detailed requirement catalog that accurately captures and defines the needs of both the OEM and the manufacturer.

We Design – Creating the Best Solution

We design, test, and validate the fasteners according to the specifications from the guiding process. This ensures that it meets the requirements of both the OEM and the manufacturer. Bossard’s approach actively tailors the final product to the precise needs and expectations outlined in the requirement catalog.
