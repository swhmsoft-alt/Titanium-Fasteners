## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement")** is entered into as of
`[DD / MONTH / YYYY]` (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Party A — Disclosing Party:** `Boze Metal`, `[Registered address]` ("Boze Metal"), and

**Party B — Receiving Party:** `[Customer legal entity name]`, `[Registered address]` ("Customer"),

each a "Party" and together the "Parties". Because this Agreement is **mutual**, each
Party may act as Disclosing Party or Receiving Party with respect to any given piece of
Confidential Information.

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate and, if commercially agreed, carry out a business
relationship for the manufacture and supply of titanium fasteners and related
components (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose
Confidential Information to the other. The Parties therefore agree as follows.

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party (the
"Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), in any form, that is designated
as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given
its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without
limitation:

- technical drawings, 2D and 3D CAD files (STEP, IGES, DXF, DWG, STL, PDF), models,
  prototypes and test pieces;
- bills of materials (BOM), part numbers, specifications, tolerances, drawings and
  engineering change notes;
- process data, manufacturability (DFM) feedback, tooling, fixture and inspection data;
- product, pricing, cost, volume, lead-time and commercial information;
- the existence and content of this Agreement and the fact that the Parties are in
  discussions;
- information marked "confidential", "proprietary", "restricted" or with an equivalent
  legend, and any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential
  given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can
demonstrate by contemporaneous written records:

1. was publicly available through no breach of this Agreement or other duty;
2. was lawfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction before disclosure by the
   Disclosing Party;
3. was received from a third party without breach of any duty of confidentiality;
4. was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the
   Disclosing Party's Confidential Information; or
5. was approved for release in writing by the Disclosing Party.

### 4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party shall:

1. use the Confidential Information **solely** for the Purpose and not for any other
   purpose, including not for competitive analysis, benchmarking or product development
   outside the Purpose;
2. restrict access to Confidential Information to those of its employees, officers,
   directors, agents and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who
   are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this
   Agreement — the Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach by them;
3. protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it applies to
   its own confidential information of the same nature, and in no event less than a
   reasonable degree of care;
4. not disclose Confidential Information to any third party, including any competitor,
   without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent;
5. not reverse engineer, disassemble, decompile or otherwise attempt to derive the
   underlying design, composition or manufacture of any physical sample or article
   received from the Disclosing Party, except to the extent such restriction is
   prohibited by law; and
6. not file or attempt to register any intellectual property right in or derived from the
   Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and not claim any invention, improvement
   or modification of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information as its own.

### 5. Required Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation or court order to disclose
Confidential Information, it shall, to the extent legally permitted, notify the
Disclosing Party in writing promptly and in advance, disclose only the portion legally
required, and cooperate (at the Disclosing Party's cost) with any effort to obtain
confidential treatment or protective relief.

### 6. Return or Destruction

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, or upon termination of the Parties'
commercial relationship, the Receiving Party shall within `10` business days return or
destroy all Confidential Information, including copies, and certify such destruction in
writing. One archival copy may be retained solely to the extent required by law or bona
fide internal compliance procedures, and shall remain subject to this Agreement.

### 7. Duration

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and remains in force for `3` years.
The obligations of confidentiality and non-use in respect of Confidential Information
shall survive termination of this Agreement for `3` years from the date of disclosure,
and, in respect of trade secrets and information constituting intellectual property,
for so long as such information remains confidential.

### 8. Return of Parts and Samples

Any physical sample, article or component supplied by the Disclosing Party shall be
returned or destroyed on request, and shall not be used for any purpose other than
evaluation under the Purpose, nor offered, sold or given away to any third party.

### 9. No Licence, No Obligation

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. No licence or
other right under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other intellectual
property right is granted by this Agreement or by any disclosure hereunder. Neither this
Agreement nor any disclosure obliges either Party to proceed with any transaction; no
partnership, agency, joint venture or employment relationship is created.

### 10. Warranty and Liability

Each Party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement. Except as
expressly stated herein, Confidential Information is provided "as is" without warranty of
any kind, and neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect or consequential
loss arising from disclosure or use of Confidential Information. Neither Party's total
liability under this Agreement shall exceed `[e.g. USD 50,000]`, save for liability that
cannot be limited by law.

### 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
`[e.g. the People's Republic of China / the Republic of Singapore / England and Wales]`,
without regard to its conflict-of-law principles. Any dispute arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the
courts of `[e.g. Shanghai / Singapore]`, without prejudice to either Party's right to
seek injunctive relief for breach of confidentiality in any competent jurisdiction.

### 12. General

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties in respect of its subject
matter and supersedes all prior discussions, proposals and understandings, whether
written or oral. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties. Neither
Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to a
successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all of its assets. If any
provision is held unenforceable, the remainder continues in full force. This Agreement may
be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an
original and all of which together constitute one agreement.

## Sending notes (internal)

   ("Confidential — Boze Metal") on every page.
2. Send as a PDF attachment (not a link) with the mail subject
   `NDA & RFQ Inquiry - Titanium Fasteners`, and reference the customer's enquiry
   address in the body.
3. Log the version and the date sent. Once countersigned, store both copies with the
   enquiry record — the file transfer may only be invited **after** both sides have signed.
